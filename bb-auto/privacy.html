TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

Your request stays a conversation.

This is an independently prepared demonstration, not the business’s official site. The public concept does not send form entries to the business or save them to a server. Dashboard entries are fictional. Do not enter sensitive information.

When the business launches its own site

The owner must approve and publish a final privacy notice identifying the business, its privacy contact and the retention period. The supplied backend has the following behavior:

  • The inquiry form collects a name, reply email or phone number, optional vehicle information, a topic and a message.
  • Submitted requests are stored in a database controlled by the business. The owner views and exports them through an authenticated dashboard.
  • Requests do not automatically book appointments or send marketing messages.
  • A necessary session cookie protects form submissions and owner sign-in. The supplied site uses no advertising trackers or third-party font services.
  • Rate limiting temporarily stores an HMAC of the connecting IP address and an attempt timestamp. Hosting providers may maintain their own access logs.
  • Clicking an external Google Maps link opens Google’s service, which has its own privacy practices.

Owner controls

The business controls its hosting, administrator account and database. It should protect backups, restrict dashboard access, set a retention schedule and provide a way to request correction or deletion of personal information. This draft must be adapted to the business’s actual practices before collecting real customer inquiries.
